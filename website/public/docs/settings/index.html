Using Dozer

Settings

Keep-awake, display, grace period and notifications.

Everything here is on the setup page (Setup… in the menu bar panel). The most-used switches are also in the panel itself.

Keep awake indefinitely

Ignores what's running and keeps your Mac awake until you turn it off. It's the old-fashioned caffeinate behaviour, for presentations or when you just want the Mac up.

Keep the display on

Off by default: Dozer stops the Mac sleeping but lets the screen turn off, which saves power and doesn't interrupt exports or downloads. Turn it on if you want to keep watching progress.

Grace period

How long Dozer keeps the Mac awake after the last bit of work ends: 10 seconds, 1 minute (the default), 5 minutes or 15 minutes. It stops your Mac dropping off between one job and the next, for example between an export finishing and you starting the upload.

Notifications

Notify me when the work is done posts an All done notification when everything Dozer was watching has finished and the Mac can sleep again.

Busy claude processes

Also treat busy claude processes as work is the fallback for Claude Code sessions that aren't connected through hooks. Leave it on unless you never use Claude Code.
