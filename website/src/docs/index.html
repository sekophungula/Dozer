Dozer is a small, free menu bar app for macOS. While your work is running, it stops your Mac from idle-sleeping. When the work finishes, it lets go, and your normal sleep settings take over again.

It's for anyone whose Mac does long jobs while they're away from the keyboard: a video editor exporting a film, a 3D artist rendering, someone downloading a big game, or a developer letting Claude Code work through a task.

Why not just change the sleep settings?

You can, but then you have to remember to change them back, and your Mac stays up all night even when the job finished an hour ago. Keep-awake timers have the same problem: you guess how long the work will take. A common trick is leaving a movie playing in the background, which works but wastes memory and slows down the export it's meant to protect.

Dozer watches the work itself, so your Mac stays awake exactly as long as it needs to.

What it watches

AppsFinal Cut Pro, Premiere, Blender and more while they export, render or build. File transfersDownloads, uploads, syncs and copies to or from external drives. Terminal & CLI agentsBuilds, scripts, Codex, Gemini and other agents. Claude CodeExact start and stop through hooks.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't stop your Mac sleeping when you close a MacBook's lid. macOS always sleeps then, unless an external display is connected.
  • It doesn't keep your Mac awake because an app is open. Apps count only while they're working hard, unless you set one to While open.
  • It doesn't send anything anywhere. No account, no analytics.
