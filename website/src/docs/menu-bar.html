The eye

IconMeans
Open eyeSomething is working, so Dozer is keeping your Mac awake.
Closed eyeDozer is on and watching. Nothing is running, so your Mac can sleep normally.
Faded closed eyeDozer is off. Your Mac sleeps as usual.

The status line

Click the eye to open the panel. The top line says what's keeping your Mac awake, for example Final Cut Pro is working, Downloading or Claude is working. Below it you'll see everything that's running and how long the Mac has been held awake.

When the work finishes you'll see Work finished with a short countdown. That's the grace period, which stops your Mac dropping off between back-to-back jobs.

The switches

SwitchWhat it does
DozerThe main switch. Off means Dozer does nothing at all.
Keep awake indefinitelyNever sleep, whatever is running. Handy for presentations. Remember to turn it off.
Watch appsStay awake while apps export, render or build. See Apps.
Watch File TransfersStay awake while files download, upload, sync or copy to or from an external drive. See File transfers.
Watch TerminalStay awake while commands and CLI agents run. See Terminal & CLI agents.
Keep display on tooAlso stop the screen turning off. Usually you don't need this: exports and downloads carry on with the screen off.

At the bottom: Setup… opens the setup page, Check for Updates looks for a new version, and Quit closes Dozer.
