Menu bar

The eye lives in the menu bar. Hold ⌘ and drag it to move it.

Control Center

On macOS 26 and later, Dozer adds a toggle to Control Center:

  1. Open Control Center and click Edit Controls.
  2. Search for Dozer and drag it in.
  3. From there you can also pin it to the menu bar.

The toggle turns Dozer on or off and shows whether it's keeping the Mac awake.

Open at login

Switch on Open Dozer at login on the setup page, so it's always watching. If macOS refuses, move Dozer into Applications and try again.
