Install

Three steps the first time.

01

Download and drag

Open Dozer.dmg and drag Dozer onto the Applications folder beside it.

02

Allow it to open

Dozer isn't notarised by Apple yet. The first time, click Done, then Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Step by step →

03

Pick what to watch

The setup page lists the export and render apps on your Mac, already switched on. Using Claude Code? Click Connect Claude Code too.
