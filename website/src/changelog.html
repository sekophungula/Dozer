November 26, 2026Latest

Waits for downloads and uploads

Version 1.5.0
  • Dozex keeps your Mac awake while big files are moving, so they finish before it sleeps.
  • Covers downloads in any browser, uploads to YouTube, Dropbox or WeTransfer, iCloud Drive or Google Drive syncing, and online backups.
  • Light traffic like music, messages or small syncs doesn't count. A transfer has to keep going for about 15 seconds first.
  • On by default. Turn it off with Watch downloads in the menu.

November 26, 2026

Watches your apps

Version 1.4.0
  • Dozex keeps your Mac awake while apps are exporting, rendering or building, not just Claude and the terminal.
  • Works with Final Cut Pro, Compressor, iMovie, Premiere Pro, Media Encoder, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, HandBrake, Logic Pro, GarageBand, Ableton Live, Pro Tools, Photoshop, Lightroom, Blender, Cinema 4D, Unity, Unreal, Xcode and Android Studio.
  • Add any other app on the setup page, and set each one to When busy, While open or Off.
  • Fewer false alarms: an idle Claude or agent session that briefly uses CPU no longer shows “Claude is working”. Work has to keep going for about 10–15 seconds to count.

November 22, 2026

Watches your terminal

Version 1.3.0
  • A running command (a build, test run, script or download) keeps your Mac awake until it finishes.
  • CLI agents like Codex, Gemini CLI, Aider and OpenCode keep it awake while they work. Left idle at the prompt, they don't.
  • Editors, ssh, tmux and REPLs that are just sitting open don't count either.
  • Works in Terminal, iTerm, Ghostty, Warp, VS Code and tmux panes. Turn it off with Watch Terminal.
