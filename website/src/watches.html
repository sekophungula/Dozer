// What it watches

Everything that keeps your Mac awake

Dozex watches four kinds of work. Each one counts only while it's actually working, so nothing sitting idle keeps your Mac up. Turn any of them off from the menu bar.

Apps

Count while they're exporting, rendering or building: about 15 seconds of hard work, including helper processes they start. Watched when installed, and you can add any other app.

Downloads & uploads

Count while 250 KB/s or more keeps moving in either direction for about 15 seconds, whichever app is doing it.

Terminal commands & CLI agents

A command counts for as long as it runs. Agents, editors and REPLs count only while they keep working. Works in Terminal, iTerm, Ghostty, Warp, VS Code and tmux.
