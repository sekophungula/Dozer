Set up in a minute

A small download for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs on macOS 14 or later. Updates install themselves from the menu bar.

01

Download and drag

Open Dozex.dmg and drag Dozex into Applications.

02

Allow it to open

Dozex isn't notarized yet. The first time, click Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security, or run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Dozex.app
03

Pick your apps

The setup page lists the export and render apps on your Mac, already switched on. Using Claude Code? Click Connect Claude Code too. Then look for the eye in your menu bar.

Questions

Can it keep my Mac awake while Final Cut Pro exports?

Yes. Dozex notices when Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Blender, HandBrake and other apps are working hard and keeps your Mac awake until they finish. No need to change your sleep settings or leave a movie playing. Any other app can be added on the setup page, and any app can be set to keep your Mac awake the whole time it's open.

Will it stop my Mac sleeping during a big download?

Yes. Downloads in any browser, uploads to YouTube, Dropbox or WeTransfer, cloud folders syncing and online backups keep your Mac awake until they finish. Light traffic like music or messages doesn't count, and a transfer has to keep going for about 15 seconds before it does.

Which agents and terminals does it work with?

Claude Code connects through hooks, in both the CLI and the Code tab of the Claude desktop app. Every other agent, including Codex, Gemini CLI, Aider and OpenCode, is detected in any terminal: Terminal, iTerm, Ghostty, Warp, VS Code and tmux panes.

Will an idle app or agent keep my Mac awake all night?

No. Apps, agents, editors, ssh, tmux and REPLs only count while they keep using CPU for a few seconds in a row, so a quick spike doesn't count. An app with a project open, or a session left at the prompt, lets your Mac sleep as usual. Regular commands like builds and tests count for as long as they run.

What happens if I close my MacBook's lid?

macOS always sleeps when the lid closes without an external display, and no app can override that without admin changes. Keep the lid open for long runs, or use an external display.

What does it change on my Mac?

It adds a few hooks to ~/.claude/settings.json, after backing up the original, and removes them if you disconnect. To stay awake it uses a standard macOS power assertion, the same one video players use.

Is it free?

Yes. Dozex is free and open source under the MIT license.
